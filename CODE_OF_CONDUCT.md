# Community guidelines

Be respectful and constructive. Critique changes and ideas, not people.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, and publishing private information
are not welcome.

Keep discussions relevant, credit others' work, and avoid spam. Assume good
intent while respecting boundaries and correcting mistakes.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict
participation. Report abuse using GitHub's private reporting tools. For software
vulnerabilities use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
